This guide helps administrators start using the Admin console in minutes, from signing in to personal settings.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open your browser and go to the system URL provided by your administrator
2. Enter your email and password, then press the sign-in button
3. If two-factor authentication is enabled, enter the full OTP code

## 2. Main menu and search

- The left menu is grouped by work area, such as dashboard, users, and AI documents
- Use the menu search box at the top to jump to the page you need
- Favorite menus (star) pin frequently opened pages to the top

## 3. Account switching and personal settings

1. Click your profile picture in the top-right corner
2. Open your profile to edit your name, email, and avatar
3. Review lingering sessions and remove devices you do not recognize

## Tips

- Switch on dark mode from the theme toggle when working at night
- You can switch between Thai and English from the language menu without signing out
