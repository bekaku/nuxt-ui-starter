The playground lets model testers compare how each model behaves before using it for real.

## 1. Starting an experiment

1. Open the Playground page and pick a gateway model
2. Choose the response style, e.g. instant answer or streaming
3. Type a test question and press send

## 2. Toggling thinking and debugging

- Turn on the thinking-steps mode when you want to see the model's reasoning
- Turn it off when you want short, fast answers
- If an answer looks off, try lowering the temperature or rewriting the prompt

## 3. Recording results

1. Note down the prompt, model, and settings used every time
2. Compare answers from at least 2-3 models before deciding
3. Hand the best prompt over to the development team for production use

## Notes

- Playground results may differ from production if the settings differ
- Do not put your organization's secrets in test questions
